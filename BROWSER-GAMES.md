# Browser Games

A click-to-play list of JoshHub games and free browser games.

This page now separates games with **current, verifiable high ratings** from games included because they are **popular, well-known, or long-running browser games**. Those are not the same thing.

## Verified highly rated

These games have a current public rating source that can be checked.

- [Infinite Craft](https://neal.fun/infinite-craft/) — Sandbox crafting game by Neal.fun. **4.7/5 from 2.7K Australian App Store ratings** (official app). [Rating source](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/infinite-craft-by-neal/id6499235533)
- [Gartic Phone](https://garticphone.com/) — Multiplayer drawing/telephone party game. **4.7 stars with 5K+ Google Play reviews** on the Australian listing. [Rating source](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_AU&id=com.gartic.garticphone)
- [Townscaper Web](https://oskarstalberg.com/Townscaper/) — Relaxing town-building toy. The same game has **95% positive English Steam reviews from 10K+ reviews**. [Rating source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1291340/Townscaper/)
- [Cookie Clicker](https://www.cookieclicker.com/) — Classic idle game. The official Steam release has **96% positive English reviews from 54K+ reviews**. [Rating source](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1454400/Cookie_Clicker/)
- [Die in the Dungeon Classic](https://alarts.itch.io/die-in-the-dungeon) — Dice-building roguelite. **4.7/5 from 2,700+ itch.io ratings**. [Rating source](https://alarts.itch.io/die-in-the-dungeon)

> Ratings can change over time. The figures above were checked on 29 September 2026.

## Popular or classic free browser games

These are included because they are established, recognisable, fun browser games or were specifically requested. I am **not** claiming that every one is currently among the highest-rated games on the web.

### FPS

- [Krunker](https://krunker.io/) — Fast low-poly multiplayer FPS
- [Venge.io](https://live.venge.io/) — Hero-based objective FPS
- [REPULS.io](https://repuls.io/) — Sci-fi arena shooter
- [Bullet Force](https://www.crazygames.com/game/bullet-force-multiplayer) — Military-style multiplayer FPS
- [Shell Shockers](https://shellshock.io/) — Multiplayer egg arena shooter

- [Slow Roads](https://slowroads.io/) — Endless relaxing procedural driving

### Puzzle and strategy

- [TETR.IO](https://tetr.io/) — Fast modern falling-block puzzle
