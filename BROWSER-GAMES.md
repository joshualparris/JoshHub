# Browser Games

A simple click-to-play list of free browser games, plus JoshHub's own playable game projects.

## JoshHub games

- [Buckland Blocks](https://buckland-blocks.vercel.app/) — Voxel sandbox
- [The Hollow Marches](https://astra-dnd-game.vercel.app/) — AI fantasy RPG
- [Car Builder + Racing](https://topsecretcheese-del.github.io/carGame/) — Car design and racing
- [Grandmaster Path](https://chess-kappa-five.vercel.app/) — Chess
- [Lantern Road](https://parris-tech-services.github.io/lantern-road/) — Adventure RPG
- [Breach Command](https://parris-tech-services.github.io/breach-command/) — Tactical roguelike
- [Whispering Wilds](https://parris-tech-services.github.io/WhirringWilderness/) — Narrative RPG
- [Wastes Courier](https://wastes-courier-roguelike.vercel.app/) — Roguelike
- [Eleven Realms](https://realms-bay.vercel.app/) — Exploration

## FPS

- [Krunker](https://krunker.io/) — Fast low-poly multiplayer FPS
- [Venge.io](https://live.venge.io/) — Hero-based objective FPS
- [REPULS.io](https://repuls.io/) — Sci-fi arena shooter
- [Bullet Force](https://www.crazygames.com/game/bullet-force-multiplayer) — Military-style multiplayer FPS
- [Shell Shockers](https://shellshock.io/) — Multiplayer egg arena shooter

## Sandbox and casual

- [Infinite Craft](https://neal.fun/infinite-craft/) — Combine elements into an enormous range of discoveries
- [Townscaper Web](https://oskarstalberg.com/Townscaper/) — Relaxing goal-free town builder
- [Slow Roads](https://slowroads.io/) — Endless relaxing procedural driving
- [Little Alchemy](https://littlealchemy.com/) — Element-combination discovery game

## Puzzle and strategy

- [TETR.IO](https://tetr.io/) — Fast modern falling-block puzzle
- [2048](https://play2048.co/) — Number-merging puzzle
- [Lichess](https://lichess.org/) — Free open-source chess
- [Die in the Dungeon Classic](https://alarts.itch.io/die-in-the-dungeon) — Dice-building roguelite
