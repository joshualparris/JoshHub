## Executive decision

Do **not** make either Men's Shed the default public Repair Café venue. Plan an independently approved accessible and inclusive venue, with volunteering opportunities for interested Shed members as mentors, woodwork/bike/hand-tool station leads or referrals. Prioritise respect for the mission of each Shed and for local commercial repairers. A public event would require its own consent, safety, child protection, accessibility and insurance verification.

## Partnership models (in order for early exploration)

1. **Neutral venue + interested individual Shed volunteers** — high inclusion potential; condition on independent insurance, approved tasks and safeguarding.
2. **Specialist woodworking/bike/mending station** — good match to publicly documented skills; station safety check essential.
3. **Mentoring, education and demonstration** — useful low-pressure pilot.
4. **Explicit referral agreement** — only after knowing the Shed's accepted items, prices, owner consent and responsibilities.
5. **Loaned tools or workshop use** — later-stage issue involving transport, operator induction and cover.
6. **Shed-hosted public Café** — consider only after full inclusion, disability access, youth, insurance and venue approval checks.

## Operational stop conditions

- No public repair activity without an agreed responsible organiser, permissions, risk assessment, suitable event insurance and consent.
- Stop potentially dangerous mains electrical and swollen/damaged lithium-ion battery jobs unless suitable accredited procedures and personnel are confirmed.
- Do not accept personal-data-bearing computers without ownership, custody and privacy safeguards.
- Check visitor age rules, Working With Children obligations, adult supervision and wheelchair/toilet access before family participation.
- Do not promise that Shed volunteers represent their organisation, or use its logos, unless its committee agrees.
- Do not publicise a partner before explicit approval.
