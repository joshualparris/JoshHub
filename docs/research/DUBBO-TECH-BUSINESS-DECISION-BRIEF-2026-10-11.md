## A. Executive briefing

- **Highest-evidence device lifecycle clues:** Orana Business Solutions advertises refurbished devices; Tech Savvy retails refurbished computers; Leading Edge advertises refurbished stock. These are evidence of businesses in the reuse trade, **not evidence of available donated stock**. [OB3](https://obsolutions.com.au/refurbished-devices), [TS1](https://www.techsavvyau.com/), [LE1](https://lecit.com.au/pages/about-us).
- **Existing arrangements matter more than hypothetical device volumes.** Colton explicitly scopes e-waste disposal separately; Viatek and Winc publish lifecycle/recycling offerings. A small independent pilot needs to be complementary and authorised rather than seeking an undocumented diversion. [CO3](https://coltoncomputers.com.au/whats-covered-under-your-managed-business-agreement/), [VI1](https://viatek.com.au/contact-us/), [WI1](https://www.winc.com.au/services/managed-print-services/).
- **Working conditions are mostly unknown.** CBM publishes unusually detailed expectations including occasional after-hours/Saturday work, documentation and outside-work conflicts; Colton and Hi Tech have public staff and recruitment evidence. Only limited employee testimony is Dubbo-specific. None proves that one workplace has a better culture. [CB2](https://cbmcomputers.com.au/join-our-team/), [CO1](https://coltoncomputers.com.au/cct-staff/), [HT1](https://htw.net.au/about/).
- **Don't mistake relationship types:** Case Indulgence has two shops; Leading Edge is in a buying group but locally independent; Orana is a Ricoh dealer, not established as Ricoh-owned; Axxis is a telecom partner, not necessarily a carrier-owned branch.
- **Best near-term value:** identify a repair specialist willing to discuss a structured referral or a small owner-released donor stream. No stock availability or willingness is verified. Approaches to any Avance-connected organisation must avoid employer client lists, confidential relationships or implicit sponsorship.

### Biggest uncertainties

Current local headcount, workplace roster, staff tenure, salaries, actual escalation software, volumes of retired devices, their legal owners, downstream ITAD contracts and anyone's authority to donate are mostly not published. Hi Tech's exact address needs confirmation before visiting. A 20-minute driving route from Boundary Road has **not been calculated**; this report does not assert one.

### Operating model and decision rule

- **Walk-in device repair:** SOS, Case Indulgence, Tech Savvy, CBM, Leading Edge — potential faults, parts, consumer referrals; customer-owned goods normally require owner authorisation.
- **Managed IT and business fleet services:** CRT, Gravity, Colton, Hi Tech — strong questions about clients' refresh cycles and managed devices, but custody is not the same as title.
- **Dealer/print contracts:** Orana, Viatek, Winc — need lease/vendor/end-of-term approval; toner/printers are different from laptops.
- **Telecommunications and POS:** Axxis, Desspos — likely specialised return or device agreements; limited direct evidence of uncommitted laptop supply.
